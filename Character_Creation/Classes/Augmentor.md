Augmentors are ingenious minds who through biological, magical, or electromechanical implants, bestow power upon those who could otherwise be completely ordinary. 

## Hit Die
Your hit die is a d6

## Starting Hitpoints
Your starting hitpoints are 6 + your constitution modifier

## Skills
You gain proficiency in 2 skills of your choice.

### Augment
You have the knowledge and skill to implant foreign objects into willing creatures over the course of a rest. You may implant an amount of Augments equal to your intelligence score per rest. These augments require resources to craft and you may only implant so many at a time. Refer to the level chart for the amount of implants you may have applied to yourself and other creatures. Refer to the list of augments at the end of this document for their effects and cost. 

### Augments Known
Each level you learn new augments, and you may learn augments by studying them in the world without leveling up. Augments are separated into three tiers, each tier being stronger than the last, with each augment having additional optional modifiers you may add to the augment based on its tier. Refer to the tiered augments known column of the level chart to see how many augments you know.

### Scrap / Harvest
You are capable of scrapping metal and harvesting flesh. You convert 2 lbs of the source (metal, flesh, etc.) into one usable scrap unit of that thing which is shelf stable and may be used to craft augments. 

### Hitpoint Increase
Your hitpoints increase by 1d6 + your constitution modifier. If your constitution modifier increases by any means, reflect that change retroactively in your maximum hp.

### Minor Hitpoint Increase
At 4th level, and every level after, add your constitution modifier to your maximum hitpoints. If your constitution modifier increases by any means, reflect that change retroactively in your maximum hp.

### Ability Score Increase
You may increase one of your ability scores by 2 or two of them by 1. You may forgo your ability score increase and take a feat instead.

### Expertise
You gain expertise in one skill you are proficient in, meaning your proficiency bonus is doubled when making checks with that skill.

### Berserker Implant
Cost: 300
Description: This implant causes a secretion of strong anger horomones, sending you into a blind rage for 1 minute. You gain a +2 to damage rolls with melee weapons. You may only use this implant once per rest.

#### Tier 2: 
Cost: 200 (500 total)
While berserk you gain resistance to bludgeoning piercing and slashing damage.
When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- You may use this augment an additional time per rest.
- While berserk your damage modifier with melee weapons is increased by 1.
- While berserk you become immune to the charmed and fear status effect.

#### Tier 3:
Cost: 500 (1000 total)
While berserk you may expend a hit die and give your next attack advantage once per turn. When enraged by a normal source, roll a wisdom saving throw against a DC 12, on a failure you accidentally activate this implant and gain the crazed status effect for one minute, you may repeat this saving throw at the end of each of your turns.
When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- You may use this augment an additional time per rest.
- While berserk your damage modifier with melee weapons is increased by 1.
- While berserk you may not be knocked prone or moved by spell effects other than teleportation.

### Aim Module
Cost: 750 Scrap
Description: This module aids your ability to aim, granting you a +2 modifier to ranged attacks.

#### Tier 2:
Cost: 250 (1000 total)
This upgrade increases the tracking of the aim module, allowing you to aim more precisely. Your effective and maximum range double with all ranged weapons.
When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- Your damage modifier with ranged weapons increases by +1
- While blinded you may make ranged attacks without disadvantage
- Ranged attacks at a target within 5 feet of you do not have disadvantage

#### Tier 3:
Cost: 350 (1350 total)
This upgrade enables the aim module at all times, greatly increasing its effectiveness. Your ranged weapon attack modifier becomes +3 and you have advantage on ranged attacks against creatures which did not move on their last turn. The module being on at all times causes your eyes to inadvertently track "possible targets" which often are normal innocent people. 
When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- Your damage modifier with ranged weapons increases by +1
- You may expend your bonus action to allow the module to tune into a creatures movement, granting you advantage on your next attack
- You may target creatures using ricochet shots without disadvantage

When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:

- You gain a +2 modifier to constitution and dexterity saving throws

#### Tier 3:
Cost: 350 (1600 total)

You become less able to resist impulsive actions, and when you are encountered by one you must make a DC 12 wisdom saving throw or act on impulse.
When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- You may use this augment an additional time per rest.
- You gain a +2 modifier to constitution and dexterity saving throws.

### Leg Replacement
Cost: 750
Description: These replacement legs are far superior to natural ones in every way. You gain an additional 20 feet of walking speed and may dash as a bonus action.

#### Tier 2:
Cost: 250 (1000 total)
You upgrade the shock absorption of your legs, allowing you to fall great distances without taking any damage. When you fall from 300 feet or less you take no fall damage, and only 1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 50 feet after that. This has the added effect of making your steps quieter, granting you advantage on stealth checks against being heard while moving.  

When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- Your walking speed increases by 10 feet
- Your jumping height/distance increases by 20 feet
- You become sure footed and may not be knocked prone

#### Tier 3:
Cost: 250 (1250 total)
Your legs allow you to move with great swiftness and grace. Your movement no longer provokes oppurtunity attacks, and you gain advantage on acrobatics checks related to jumping.

When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- Your walking speed increases by 10 feet
- Your jumping height/distance increases by 20 feet
- When a creature attempts to knock you prone and fails you may expend a reaction to move half your movement speed in any direction.

### Prehensile Tail
Cost: 200
Description: This prehensile tail taps onto and extends your spinal cord. You may use items and one-handed light weapons with this tail with an additional 5 feet of reach. You may also hang from structures with it. 

#### Tier 2:
Cost: 200 (400 total)
You extend your tail and make it stronger, granting it an additonal 5 feet of reach and allowing it to hold any one handed weapon and lift your weight granting you 20 feet of climbing speed.

When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- Weapons held by your tail gain the finesse property.
- You may expend 5 feet of movement speed to stand from prone as your tail pushes you up.
- When you take the attack action you may expend a bonus action to make a contested athletics check against another creature, shoving them prone with your tail on a success.

#### Tier 3:
Cost: 200 (600 total)
You re-inforce your tail, allowing you to take defensive actions with it. As a reaction to being targeted by an attack you may whip your tail to block and add your proficiency modifier to your AC, possibly making the attack miss. Your tail begins to flick and move on its own, seemingly acting of its own will at times. 

When you learn this tier of augment choose one benefit from the following:
- Finesse weapons held by your tail gain a +2 modifier to attack and damage rolls.
- When you stand from prone you may immediately make an attack at any creature within 5 feet of you with your tail, as you swing it with your standing momentum.
- When you knock a creature prone you may use your reaction to attempt to bind them, forcing them to make a DC 15 strength saving throw or be restrained by your tail.
